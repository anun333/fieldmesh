# Code of conduct

This project exists for farmers, many of whom are not programmers and many of whom do not
speak English. Contributions are judged on what they do for them.

* Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to software, to farming, or to English.
* Argue about evidence and designs, not about people.
* Credit others' work and data.
* No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind, here or in any project space.

Problems can be raised privately with the maintainer through GitHub (@anun333). They are
handled confidentially; someone who breaks these rules may be asked to stop or removed from
the project's spaces.
