# The same farm, 2050s

The site's own thirty years of weather, every day +1.0 °C warmer and with +0% rain — the delta method: today's ups and downs kept, their level moved. It does not model nights warming faster than days. Where the change comes from: the Berkeley Earth trend for Tennessee in ~/dev/climate-analysis: +1.89 °C by 2050 against 1951–1980, about +1.0 °C against this 1991–2020 record.
